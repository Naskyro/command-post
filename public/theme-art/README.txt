COMMAND ORIGINAL SEASONAL ARTWORK

The SVG files here are the original motif library, retained for artwork editing and handoff. The current shell and theme picker share the renderer in components/preferences/ThemeDecorations.tsx.

SeasonalIllustrations.tsx holds the illustrated motifs and the natural-proportion repeating Christmas garland. ElegantSeasonalArt.tsx renders fine-line ornaments and friezes. IndependenceArt.tsx contains ten original Independence Day compositions. The Nature & Realistic collection uses local WebP images in public/theme-photography. Classic Color renders no decorative art.

Decorations do not capture pointer input, are hidden from assistive technology, and are not printed.
