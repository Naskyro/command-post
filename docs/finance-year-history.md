# Finance: Archive & Year in Review

Open **Finance → Reports & Taxes → Archive & Year in Review**. Staff need the Reports & Taxes grant and the relevant source permission. A user with just My Pay, Payroll, or a new-hire account cannot use this page to obtain payroll or register details. Viewing a year performs no writes; it does not close accounting periods, dispose of attachments, archive account balances, or create journal entries.

| Record | Activity and year | What contributes to the money totals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bank transaction | The business date on the register entry. Imported statement entries, Posted, Cleared, Categorized, Matched, and Reconciled describe recorded movements. Pending/Draft/Scheduled items are not posted cash. A previous-year posting remains editable in Bookkeeping even when listed in annual history. | Integer cents of each identifiable posted transaction: positive inflow, negative outflow, zero neutral. These are gross visible register movements, including transfers; they do not measure revenue, expenses, or profit. |
| Vendor bill | Opens on bill date. Later partial payments count as activity but do not close the bill. Final payment/settlement date or a recorded cancellation/closure determines the archive year. A complete, dated payment history covering the recorded paid balance can establish the date a paid bill finished settling. | Count only. Paying the bill may already be reflected in the bank register. |
| Client invoice | Opens on invoice date. An explicit final settlement or cancellation date establishes closure. A due date, updated timestamp or editable paid balance is not evidence of when payment occurred. | Count only; use the Receivables reports for billed amounts/open balances. |
| Release receipt / cash movement / debt payment | Uses the business date on the respective receipt or recorded movement. Only a known completed status establishes completion. | Count only. A deposit, debt repayment, internal transfer, withdrawal, or release charge may already appear in the register. |
| Payroll lines, employee pay records, account/debt balance snapshots, tax returns, journals | Keep their dedicated workflows, access controls and reports. The annual lookup does not reproduce them. | Not a second accounting source. Explicit payroll-linked or payroll-classified register entries are excluded. Unclassified bank transactions have no personal or memo detail in this projection. |

The signed amount is taken from the source transaction. A stored correction or deletion with the same stable bank ID overrides the imported version; matching by day/amount would incorrectly erase real purchases. Missing amounts are empty. Dates without an explicit business day or timestamp with a timezone are not inferred. A paid record missing its settlement date can still appear in an activity year if its issue date is known; it is also identified under missing dates and is not automatically archived under the issue year.

Open invoices and bills carry forward across December 31. The invoice list defaults to open and current-year records and retains **All Years** for editing prior records. Bills already have an Unpaid queue and a status filter. Calendar years follow Austin time. As of the source snapshot, the imported register contains 5,547 dated entries from January 2, 2025 through September 3, 2026; later saves may extend or correct those dates. The endpoints are not proof that every bank/account/month is represented. For closing balances and financial statements, use the Finance ledger and its original reports.
