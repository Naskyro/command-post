# People & HR implementation — October 26, 2026

Historical implementation note. The October 27 update in `hr-update-2026-10-27.md` supersedes the comparison feature, navigation, bank setup, job-description rendering and checklist-completion descriptions below. ADP comparison was removed; it is not a current feature.

## Purpose and approved decisions

Replace the former largely illustrative HR screens with persistent, permission-scoped personnel workflows. The company is growing and people currently wear several hats. Assign responsibilities to users through Admin, rather than embedding a particular manager's name in workflow code.

The owner approved a designated onboarding approver; no E-Verify enrollment; no discretionary company benefits for part-time, temporary, or seasonal workers; PTO on eligible worked hours capped at 40 per workweek; manager-documented refusal to sign; continued Timeclock access for existing workers with missing historical documents; and continued recording of actual work after an approved-hours limit is reached. Payroll is being tested in comparison mode, not switched from ADP.

## Navigation and daily work

Eight primary areas: Home, People, Recruitment & Onboarding, Performance, Compensation & Benefits, Training & Compliance, Reports, Settings. Single-row navigation retains the active-tab triangle and overflow controls. People supports search, optional filters, per-user column preferences, current/former staff and an org chart. Dashboard and queue counts come from saved records rather than named mock personnel cases.

Employee profiles group Overview, Onboarding, Performance, Documents, Compensation & Benefits, Schedule & Attendance, Role Changes and Offboarding. Private management conversations are separate from published corrective actions. The removed dispatcher Quality & Accuracy feature is not reintroduced.

## Departments and positions

Authorized Admin users can create, rename and retire departments. Department identity is stable; name changes preserve aliases so existing assignments and permission logic continue to resolve. New assignments use active departments. Retired departments remain available in history. Department head and backup assignments are editable and audited. Changing the Operations head cannot be used by an ordinary settings delegate to grant themselves payroll release authority.

Positions are a configurable HR catalog. Do not use department names or position titles as universal permission grants. Company-wide catalog changes require company scope. Historical source records are not mass-rewritten after a rename.

## Permissions

The HR responsibility registry is in `lib/hr/permissions.ts`. Each assignment contains an action and scope: Self, Team, Department or Company. Responsibilities include personnel administration, hiring, start approval, I-9 verification, screening review, tax/bank review, compensation, benefits, performance notes, corrective actions, positions, policies, offboarding, exports, TWC work and settings.

Module/tab access alone does not grant confidential HR duties. Server-side checks enforce responsibility plus scope. Employee list projections exclude pay and confidential values unless the caller has the separate capability. Employees cannot read private management notes, draft disciplinary notices, screening reports or offboarding decisions. A user cannot perform their own employer I-9 verification, even if an owner.

## Onboarding

New Hire, Assisted New Hire and Existing Employee paths are distinct. New hires receive an onboarding-only session and cannot use ordinary modules or clock in until authorized clearance. Existing employees retain timekeeping while missing legacy paperwork stays in the HR queue. Newly added directory-only staff also receive the onboarding gate; manually enabling their account does not silently waive it.

Employee and employer tasks share a progress checklist. Offer and job-description PDFs are generated from saved terms, uploaded as exact files, and signed against the file hash. A change to those terms invalidates readiness based on an older document without deleting the signed history. Assisted onboarding supports actual wet-signed uploads with authorized verification. Job descriptions retain FLSA and duty details.

Readiness checks supporting records, not just checked boxes: signed offer/JD; employee I-9 verification; verified W-4 or contractor W-9; verified bank setup or approved check payment; pay terms; drug result; background result or a pending background review with assigned follow-up; and orientation. Start approval atomically updates HR clearance and the operational person record with version checks on supporting evidence.

The hosting platform currently requires ChatGPT sign-in. A candidate's offer link cannot precede all authentication on this hosting configuration. Existing Site audience settings are preserved. No invitation email is automatically sent by this update.

## Documents and government forms

PDF generators cover offer letters, job descriptions, corrective actions, onboarding progress and the supplied drug-screen request. The 43 HR-CA policy references are transcribed from the provided version 3 policy, effective July 1; custom published references can also be selected. The policy title becomes the corrective-action subject and its version is preserved.

I-9, W-4 and W-9 use official form links, uploaded signed source forms, structured entries and employer verification. This release does not claim to be a complete native electronic government-form signing system. No E-Verify submission is made. I-9 verification records physical document examination. Direct-deposit setup validates ABA routing structure and stores full account information only in protected HR storage. No bank payment or ACH transmission is initiated.

## Performance and personnel history

Quick conversations and verbal warnings require a category, event date and factual summary. The occurrence date can be historical; creation and actor timestamps cannot be backdated by the form. Saved notes are preserved and corrections use a new record/addendum.

Corrective actions support policy selection, expectations, consequences, review date, PDF generation, signed-copy upload, signature verification and release to the employee. Release requires the verified signed file to match the current notice version. Employee responses are appended. A disagreement can use a selected reason and/or text. A manager may record a refusal, but refusal is not recorded as a signature and does not satisfy the signed-copy publication gate. General internal evidence does not become employee-visible merely because a notice is published.

TWC tasks are assigned through their own responsibility. Authorized staff can use dated personnel history, formal notices and offboarding details for response preparation. This update does not automatically submit a TWC response. Offboarding records a checklist, separation date/reason and rehire eligibility; employment status and account access require their respective authorized controls rather than treating checklist completion as an automatic termination action.

## Time, leave and pay

Paid breaks remain recorded work. Additional Hours requests support partial approval and salaried-exempt requests without automatically turning them into overtime wages or comp-time credits. At the approved weekly limit the interface displays Approved Hours Limit Reached and creates a supervisor-visible notice, while the running clock continues to record actual work. Authorization and wages remain distinct records.

The PTO calculation uses approved worked hours, includes paid breaks, excludes paid leave and caps eligible hours at 40 per workweek. Employment class and hire date must be known; missing classifications are not silently treated as benefit-eligible. Policy rates follow service anniversaries. This is a preview and does not automatically credit the PTO bank. A reviewed opening balance and accrual start boundary are required before historical posting is safe. Automatic sick/personal grants, rollover and full benefit-ledger automation remain separate work.

Part-time, temporary and seasonal ordinary time-off requests use unpaid time and approved shift coverage. Protected Leave Review is a separate route; it is not denied by the ordinary coverage requirement and is not automatically paid.

The isolated 2026 Texas payroll comparison engine uses current IRS 15-T withholding methods, FICA and employer tax provisions. It requires actual W-4 elections, taxable wage bases, YTD amounts and the company's TWC rate. Unsupported scenarios fail validation. ADP amounts can be entered for variance comparisons; snapshots can be exported. The calculator does not post payroll, release pay, send payments, file taxes or replace the existing live payroll engine. Finance retains the existing effective compensation functionality. Benefit selections and contribution/override records do not by themselves prove insurance carrier enrollment or initiate contributions.

## Storage, audit and migration

New API routes: `/api/hr`, `/api/hr/files`, `/api/hr/payroll-preview`. D1 migration `0006_chilly_whistler.sql` creates `hr_records`, `hr_audit` and `hr_write_guard`. HR record payloads, audit snapshots and uploaded file bytes use AES-GCM encryption with the runtime secret `HR_DATA_ENCRYPTION_KEY` (base64-encoded 32 bytes). Do not place the secret in source, browser bundles or exported reports. It must be retained securely; replacing it without a key migration makes existing records unreadable.

Identity comes from the established server-verified session. Mutations use optimistic versions and sensitive actions have server-side authorization. Encrypted evidence is accessed through authenticated routes. Read/download events are audited. Deployment adds schema but does not bulk-import historic employee scans, overwrite personnel records, send invitations or change the Site audience.
