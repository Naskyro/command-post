Grading Rubric for VSF Employee Training Scenarios
Criteria Overview
Each criterion is scored on a scale of 1-5, with 5 being exemplary and 1 being poor. The total score reflects the employee’s ability to handle customer interactions professionally, empathetically, and effectively while adhering to rules and procedures.
________________


Grading Criteria
                                                1. Professionalism
                                                * 1: Displays an unprofessional tone, becomes defensive, or argues with the customer.
                                                * 2: Demonstrates some professionalism but uses dismissive or curt language.
                                                * 3: Maintains a neutral tone but does not actively de-escalate or build rapport.
                                                * 4: Uses a calm and professional tone throughout but misses opportunities to enhance the interaction.
                                                * 5: Exemplifies professionalism, remains calm, respectful, and focused on resolution even in difficult situations.
________________


                                                2. Authority
                                                * 1: Does not demonstrate understanding of their authority or backs down unnecessarily.
                                                * 2: Struggles to explain policies or enforces them without clarity.
                                                * 3: Enforces rules appropriately but with minimal confidence or clarity.
                                                * 4: Clearly communicates policies and enforces them fairly and confidently.
                                                * 5: Demonstrates complete confidence in explaining and enforcing policies while remaining approachable and professional.
________________


                                                3. Empathy
                                                * 1: Shows no concern for the customer’s emotions or circumstances.
                                                * 2: Acknowledges emotions but does not respond effectively to the customer’s frustrations.
                                                * 3: Displays empathy but struggles to balance it with enforcing policies.
                                                * 4: Effectively balances empathy with fairness, acknowledging customer concerns while maintaining professionalism.
                                                * 5: Goes above and beyond to demonstrate understanding and compassion while still adhering to the rules.
________________


                                                4. Knowledge of Rules & Procedures
                                                * 1: Lacks understanding of rules, provides incorrect information, or cannot explain policies.
                                                * 2: Demonstrates basic knowledge but struggles to apply or communicate rules effectively.
                                                * 3: Understands policies but misses opportunities to fully explain them or lacks clarity.
                                                * 4: Accurately explains and applies policies but could be more thorough or confident.
                                                * 5: Displays expert knowledge of policies, explains them clearly, and applies them seamlessly to the situation.
________________


                                                5. Customer Service
                                                * 1: Does not attempt to assist the customer or resolve the issue.
                                                * 2: Attempts to assist but uses poor communication or fails to address the customer’s main concerns.
                                                * 3: Provides basic assistance but lacks a proactive approach to customer needs.
                                                * 4: Effectively assists the customer and ensures clarity but misses opportunities for exceptional service.
                                                * 5: Delivers exceptional service by addressing concerns, providing clear next steps, and ensuring the customer feels heard and supported.
________________


Scenarios Application
Scenario A: Customer Service
                                                * Focus: Professionalism, Empathy, and Knowledge of Procedures.
                                                * Example Response Scoring: Employee calmly explains the process, acknowledges frustration, and focuses on resolving the issue.
Scenario B: All Attitude
                                                * Focus: Professionalism, Authority, and Customer Service.
                                                * Example Response Scoring: Employee avoids arguing or dismissing the customer, instead redirecting the conversation to productive next steps.
Scenario C: Empathy
                                                * Focus: Empathy and Professionalism.
                                                * Example Response Scoring: Employee listens actively, validates emotions, and offers constructive solutions while adhering to policies.
Scenario D: Legal
                                                * Focus: Knowledge of Rules, Authority, and Professionalism.
                                                * Example Response Scoring: Employee calmly explains legal obligations, maintains professionalism under pressure, and provides clear information.
Scenario E: Safety
                                                * Focus: Professionalism, Authority, and Knowledge of Procedures.
                                                * Example Response Scoring: Employee prioritizes safety, calmly disengages when threatened, and follows protocols for escalating the situation.
Scenario F: Payment
                                                * Focus: Knowledge of Procedures, Empathy, and Professionalism.
                                                * Example Response Scoring: Employee explains payment policies clearly and calmly while acknowledging the customer’s frustration.
Scenario G: Customer Call
                                                * Focus: Customer Service, Knowledge of Procedures.
                                                * Example Response Scoring: Employee verifies vehicle details, provides accurate information, and ensures the customer understands the process.
Scenario H: Customer 2nd Call
                                                * Focus: Empathy, Knowledge of Procedures, and Customer Service.
                                                * Example Response Scoring: Employee explains fees clearly, validates the customer’s concerns, and provides helpful guidance.
